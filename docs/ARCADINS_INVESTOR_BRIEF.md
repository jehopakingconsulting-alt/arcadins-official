## The opportunity
Immigration to Canada requires proof of French for many pathways (Express Entry, PEQ, MIFI), driving
sustained demand for **TEF/TCF preparation** among a global Francophone audience. ARCADINS addresses this
with a **specialized, honest, self‑service online platform** — plus a professional‑training arm for
Canadian‑context career skills.

## What already exists (the asset)
A **production‑accepted, security‑hardened web platform** (Next.js 16 + Supabase + Stripe on Vercel):
- Public platform live; two clean product lines (language vs professional).
- Complete TEF/TCF program experience + 9 professional programs.
- **Built‑but‑dormant** revenue and learning engines: self‑service checkout (+ installments + BNPL),
  learning runtime (lessons, exams, certificates), analytics — all tested, awaiting activation.
- Strong engineering discipline: 740 automated tests, clean codebase, documented, RC1‑certified.

**Interpretation:** the hard build is largely done. The near‑term plan is **activation** (turn on
commerce + learning), not a multi‑year green‑field effort — a materially de‑risked execution path.

## Business model
Direct‑to‑consumer online education. Revenue from **language packages** ($97–$347 USD) and
**professional trainings** (CA$1,500 + CA$100 one‑time registration), with **flexible financing**
(1×/3×/6× or Klarna/Affirm/Afterpay) to lift conversion. Future: coaching, certificates,
corporate/institutional licensing (multi‑tenant seam already in the schema).

## Market
Global Francophone prospective immigrants + newcomers to Canada. High‑intent, recurring inflow driven
by immigration policy. *(Precise TAM/SAM/SOM to be validated; not asserted here.)*

## Differentiation
- **Specialization** in TEF/TCF + immigration context (vs generalist MOOCs).
- **Honesty as a moat:** no visa/result guarantees, no fabricated proof — trust‑first positioning in a
  category prone to over‑promising.
- **Automation‑ready:** self‑service journey that scales without manual admissions.

## Traction
**None yet — pre‑launch.** This is stated plainly. The near‑term milestone is the **first paying cohort**
via the V3 commerce activation; investor conversations should be milestone‑based (first revenue,
retention, completion), not on assumed numbers.

## Use of funds (illustrative priorities, not a fixed ask)
1. Activate commerce + payments (finish the last mile; owner Stripe access + test‑mode E2E).

## Key risks (and mitigations)
- **Execution/activation risk** → engine already built + tested; milestone‑gated activation.
- **Regulatory/claims risk** → strict honesty, disclaimers, no guarantees.
- **Trust/no‑proof risk** → build verifiable outcomes from real cohorts; never fabricate.
- **Competition** → niche focus + transparency.

## The ask (framing)
A **milestone‑based seed/pre‑seed** conversation tied to activating revenue and proving a first cohort —
**not** a large raise against assumed traction. The platform asset and honest positioning are the
foundation; the next proof point is live revenue.
